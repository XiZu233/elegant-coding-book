<div class="conclusion-poem">

<p class="title">结 语</p>
<p class="subtitle">— Coda —</p>

<div class="stanza">
<div class="zh">
<p>在 AI 时代，</p>
<p>「不会编程」</p>
<p>与「不会用 AI 编程」</p>
<p>是两种截然不同的状态。</p>
</div>
<div class="en">
<p>In the age of AI,</p>
<p><em>not knowing how to code</em></p>
<p>and <em>not knowing how to code with AI</em></p>
<p>are two entirely different states.</p>
</div>
</div>

<hr class="divider">

<div class="stanza">
<div class="zh">
<p>你不必成为</p>
<p>记诵语法的机器，</p>
<p>你要做</p>
<p>清晰表达问题的人，</p>
<p>严格验收成果的人，</p>
<p>懂得架构设计的人。</p>
</div>
<div class="en">
<p>You need not become</p>
<p>a machine that memorizes syntax —</p>
<p>be instead</p>
<p>the one who frames the question,</p>
<p>the one who tests the answer,</p>
<p>the one who shapes the whole.</p>
</div>
</div>

<hr class="divider">

<div class="stanza">
<div class="zh">
<p>代码不过是</p>
<p>承载想法的媒介，</p>
<p>而 AI，</p>
<p>让这媒介前所未有地</p>
<p>平易近人。</p>
</div>
<div class="en">
<p>Code is merely</p>
<p>the medium for an idea,</p>
<p>and AI</p>
<p>has made that medium</p>
<p>kinder than ever before.</p>
</div>
</div>

<hr class="divider">

<div class="stanza">
<div class="zh">
<p>优雅编程的真谛 ——</p>
<p>用最少的精力，</p>
<p>创造最可靠的价值，</p>
<p>怀着对技术的敬畏</p>
<p>与热爱。</p>
</div>
<div class="en">
<p>The art of elegant coding —</p>
<p>with the least effort,</p>
<p>to make the most trustworthy thing,</p>
<p>holding both reverence</p>
<p>and love for the craft.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p class="signature">— 写于 AI 编程时代之初 · Written at the dawn of the AI age —</p>

</div>
